Discover the Power of Millets

Ancient grains for a modern lifestyle. Explore the incredible nutritional density, ecological benefits, and versatile culinary applications of these sustainable superfoods.

Types of Millets

High Calcium

Ragi (Finger Millet)

Known for its cooling properties and exceptional calcium content. Perfect for porridges and rotis.

Iron Rich

Bajra (Pearl Millet)

A winter staple packed with iron and energy. Traditionally paired with jaggery and ghee.

Gluten Free

Jowar (Sorghum)

A versatile, globally cultivated grain. Excellent for baking and flatbreads due to its neutral flavor.

Antioxidants

Foxtail Millet

Rich in antioxidants and dietary fiber. A great rice substitute with a mildly nutty taste.

Nutritional Powerhouse

Millets are vastly superior to refined grains, offering a dense profile of essential macro and micronutrients crucial for vitality.
